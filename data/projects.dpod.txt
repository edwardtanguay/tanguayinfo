compoutput
Comprehensible Ouput
https://comprehensible-output.vercel.app
This is a site I use to learn numerous languages at once including German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Polish. The goal is to produce enough text in these target languages everyday in order to record an audio reading them out load that lasts at least 5 minutes.
